Spot network attacks as they happen.

CRACKA NIDS reads live network packets and uploaded traffic logs, then labels each one as normal traffic or one of four attack types: DoS, Probe, U2R and R2L.

What it does

Two models, one decision

A Random Forest and a neural network each score the traffic. Their probabilities are averaged, which is steadier than trusting either one alone.

Live packet capture

Scapy captures packets in one-second windows and turns them into the 41 features the models were trained on. Alerts play a sound and show a desktop notification.

CSV batch analysis

Upload a traffic log in NSL-KDD format and get a label and confidence for every row, plus a count per attack type.
